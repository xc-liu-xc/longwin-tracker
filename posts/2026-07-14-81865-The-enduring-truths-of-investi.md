# The enduring truths of investi

The enduring truths of investing：

the need for second-level thinking, the importance of risk control, the inevitability of cycles, and the futility of macro forecasting.

投资亘古不变的核心真理：

第二层思维的必要性；风险控制的重要性；周期的必然性；宏观预测的徒劳无用。
